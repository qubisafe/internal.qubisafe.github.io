⚖️ Code of Conduct

Our shared standards for professional, ethical behaviour. Updated June 2026.

✅
All employees must acknowledge this policy annually. Your last acknowledgement: Jan 15, 2026.

1. Professional Conduct

We treat every colleague, client, and partner with respect. Discriminatory, harassing, or threatening behaviour of any kind is not tolerated — whether in person, in writing, or in digital channels. Concerns should be raised with your manager or HR.

2. Conflict of Interest

Avoid situations where personal interests could conflict with your duties. Disclose to HR any outside employment, investments in competitors, or personal relationships that may influence business decisions. When in doubt, disclose.

3. Confidentiality

Protect proprietary information — client data, financial forecasts, product roadmaps, and personnel matters. This obligation continues after employment ends. Do not share confidential information on personal devices or unapproved channels.

4. Integrity & Honesty

Accurate record-keeping, honest communication, and fair dealing are non-negotiable. Falsifying records, misrepresenting work, or accepting undisclosed gifts over $50 in value is a disciplinary matter.

5. Reporting Concerns

Report violations through your manager, HR, or the anonymous Ethics Hotline at ethics@yourorg.com. Retaliation against anyone who reports in good faith is strictly prohibited.

💻 Remote Work Policy

Guidelines for working outside the office. Updated May 2026.

📌

Eligibility

Employees who have completed their 3-month probation and whose roles do not require on-site presence may work remotely up to 3 days per week.

🕙

Core Hours

All employees must be reachable 10 AM–3 PM in their local timezone on working days, regardless of remote or office status.

🏠

Home Office Setup

The company provides a one-time home office allowance of $600 for equipment. Submit receipts within 60 days via Expense Report (IT Equipment category).

🔒

Security

Always use the company VPN when accessing internal systems. Do not work from unsecured public Wi-Fi without VPN enabled. Lock screens when stepping away.

📅

In-Office Days

Mandatory office attendance for All-Hands events, onboarding sessions, and any day explicitly called by your manager with 48 hours' notice.

🌍

International Remote

Working remotely from another country for more than 10 consecutive days requires prior approval from HR and Legal. Tax implications vary.

🚀 Onboarding Guide

Everything a new joiner needs in their first 30 days.

👋
Welcome! Complete all checklist items within your first 2 weeks. Your buddy is your first point of contact for any questions.

📅 Day 1 — Setup

Collect laptop from IT. Set up email, Slack, and 1Password. Complete mandatory security training in LMS. Meet your manager for a 30-min intro call.

📅 Week 1 — Orientation

Attend the New Joiner Orientation session (HR-scheduled). Review all policies in this portal and sign the acknowledgement form in Workday. Schedule 1:1s with your direct teammates.

📅 Weeks 2–4 — Ramp-up

Shadow relevant team members. Complete role-specific training in LMS. Set 30-60-90 day goals with your manager. Join relevant Slack channels and recurring meetings.
